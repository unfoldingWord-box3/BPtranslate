Short answers to common questions. Each answer links to the article with the full steps.

## I signed in but I see "Not authorized". What do I do?
Your Door43 account is not on the editor list yet. Ask an admin to add you. If you used the wrong account, choose **Sign in with a different Door43 account**. See [Getting started](#help:getting-started).

## How do I change the language of the menus?
Open your account menu (the round button at the top right) and choose **Interface language**. This changes the buttons and menus, not the text you translate. See [Getting started](#help:getting-started).

## A book says "Not imported" and I cannot open it. Why?
An admin has to bring the book into the app first. Ask your admin to open **Books**, select the book and choose **Bring in**. See [Books](#help:books).

## I typed a note but the top bar says "unsaved". Is my work lost?
No. Your text is kept in your browser, and it comes back when you return to that note. Select **Save** (or **Save & Approve**) to send it. See [Saving and working offline](#help:save-and-sync).

## Do my edits go straight to Door43?
No. They go to the app's own database first. Once a night the app publishes the approved content to Door43. See [Saving and working offline](#help:save-and-sync).

## What happens if my internet connection drops?
Keep working. Each edit you save waits in a queue in your browser and is sent when the connection returns. See [Saving and working offline](#help:save-and-sync).

## Another person edited the same note. What now?
The app does not overwrite their work. A banner or window tells you, and you choose how to keep your version or load theirs. See [Saving and working offline](#help:save-and-sync).

## What does Approve do?
Approve marks a note, question or verse as finished and checked. Only approved notes and questions are published to Door43. See [Notes](#help:notes).

## How do I get a new AI draft for a note?
On the Notes screen, select **Redo**. The AI works in the background and the new draft appears when it is ready. See [Notes](#help:notes).

## Some words in my verse became "unaligned". Why?
Changing words in a verse can remove their link to the original words. Open the Alignment screen and link them again. See [Word alignment](#help:alignment).

## How can I see the Hebrew or Greek behind a word?
Open the Verse view and click the word. The panel shows the original word and its dictionary entry. See [Verse view](#help:verse-view).

## Where is the old three-column editor?
Open your account menu and choose **Classic editor**. See [Classic editor](#help:classic-editor).

## How do I add someone to my team?
Admins do this in the Admin area. See [Team](#help:admin-team).
